# LMNT TTS — 2026-09-01

`blizzard` remains LMNT's only model and the right default; the branch fills in
10 unmapped languages, and the open question is when to move off LMNT's
deprecated 1.0 session protocol.

## What's new for Pipecat

### PRs
- https://github.com/pipecat-ai/pipecat/pull/5544 — Map the full LMNT language set for LmntTTSService

### To consider
- **Deprecated 1.0 session protocol** (high, since 2026-09-01) — LMNT's versioning page states every request must carry an `lmnt-version` and that "previous versions are considered deprecated and may be unavailable to new users". `_connect_websocket` sends none, uses the pre-1.1 untyped frames (`{"text": ...}`, `{"flush": true}`) and a `model` field that 1.1 removed as a legacy option; `eof` is already commented out because the 1.0 form errors. Migrating to 1.1/1.2 would replace that workaround with `finish`, give `flush_complete` acks (today end-of-audio is inferred from the stop-frame timeout — a 3.5 s wait in the probes below), give `reset` as a cheap alternative to reconnecting on interruption, and surface `request_id` and the structured error envelope. Synthesis works today on this account's key. — *Needs a call: whether LMNT still accepts 1.0 for newly issued keys, and who owns the protocol rewrite — it is more than a value change.*
- **Word timestamps** (medium, since 2026-09-01) — `return_timestamps` in the 1.1 session init, with cumulative audio-aligned `start` times as of 1.2, would let `LmntTTSService` report word timestamps the way the Cartesia and ElevenLabs services do. — *Needs a call: the timestamps arrive on a receive message the 1.0 protocol does not carry, so this rides on the API-version migration rather than being a standalone Settings field.*

## Verification

`probe.py run` cannot complete for `LmntTTSService`: with no output transport in
the harness there is no `BotStoppedSpeakingFrame` to lift the pause that
`TTSService` takes after a turn, so the `EndFrame` never lands and the run hangs
(reproduced past 200 s). This is the harness, not LMNT — it will hit every TTS
service constructed with `pause_frame_processing=True`. Latency below comes from
the same service metrics via a minimal pipeline in `scratch_dir` that reads the
service's own TTFB and counts pushed audio.
