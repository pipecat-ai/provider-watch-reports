# Google Image — 2026-11-23

Nothing new to ship: the service generates images through `generate_content` as it stands, and the only change since the last run is in our favour — the google-genai pin now admits 3.x, closing that gap. The default is still deprecated and its successor is still the slower model, by more than before.

## What's new for Pipecat

### PRs

Nothing.

### To consider

- **Default model deprecated** (high, since 2026-10-12) — Google deprecated `gemini-3.1-flash-image` on 2026-10-06 in favour of `gemini-nano-banana-2.1`; the deprecations page still names 2.1 as its replacement, still lists no shutdown date, and the image-generation page still tells new projects to use 2.1. The successor needs no code change, but today's re-probe puts it at 12.2 s median against 7.6 s for the current default over three interleaved rounds (~60%, against ~35% on 2026-11-09 and ~20% on 2026-10-12). The direction has held across three runs, so this is the model, not the sample. `gemini-3.1-flash-lite-image` remains undeprecated and is the fastest option, at lower quality and without multi-reference input, multi-turn editing or Search grounding. — *Needs a call: whether a ~60% slower generation is an acceptable price for moving off a deprecated default, or whether the much faster lite model is the better target for a still-frame bot.*
- **Remaining image controls not exposed** (low, since 2026-09-01) — `GoogleImageGenSettings` covers `aspect_ratio` and `image_size`, both confirmed working today (16:9 at 2K returned a 2752×1536 frame on both models). Of the rest of `types.ImageConfig`, `output_mime_type` and `output_compression_quality` carry the SDK's own "not supported in Gemini API", and `image_output_options` (new in the installed 3.2.0) says the same, which leaves `person_generation` and `prominent_people`. Beyond `ImageConfig`, the docs describe a `thinking_level` and Google Search grounding for image models — both expressible on `generate_content` via `thinking_config` and `tools` — that the service never sends. — *Needs a call: which of these users actually ask for; neither person control appears on Google's image-generation page, and the thinking levels differ per model and are billed, so exposing them needs a stance on validation and on models that reject a value.*
- **Interactions is the documented surface** (low, since 2026-11-09) — the image-generation page shows only `client.interactions.create` / `POST /v1beta/interactions`. This is still positioning, not a retirement: the migration guide repeats that generateContent "remains fully supported" and gives no date, and all eight probe calls today went through `generate_content`. What the service cannot reach from where it stands is multi-turn editing via `previous_interaction_id`, video as image context, and `image_search` grounding. — *Needs a call: whether to stay on generate_content or plan a port to interactions, which is a rewrite of `run_image_gen` rather than a settings change.*

Last run's `google-genai 3.0.0 is outside our <3 pin` gap is closed: `pyproject.toml` now pins `google-genai>=3.2.0,<4`, and PyPI's latest 3.6.1 (2026-11-18) is inside it.
