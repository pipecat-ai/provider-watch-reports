# Google LLM — 2026-09-01

`gemini-3.6-flash` stays the default: aiewf-eval has not benchmarked `gemini-3.7-flash`, and a `--repeat 5` comparison puts it ~25% behind 3.6 at the median TTFAT. Nothing is broken; no branch this run.

## What's new for Pipecat

### PRs
Nothing.

### To consider
- **gemini-3.7-flash as the default** (medium, since 2026-08-25) — GA on 2026-08-13, positioned as Google's latest stable Flash model, and Pipecat already carries its `"low"` thinking floor. Two things block the bump. aiewf-eval's medium-context leaderboard has no `gemini-3.7-flash` row, while `gemini-3.6-flash (minimal)` scores 97.1% pass / 798 ms TTFAT P50 — so there is nothing to compare quality against. And this run's `--repeat 5` measurement (which supersedes last run's noisy single samples, including the 14 s outlier) shows 3.7 slower at the median on both a trivial and a reasoning prompt, with a much wider spread. — *Needs a call: hold until aiewf-eval scores 3.7 Flash at or above 3.6, or is the coding/agentic gain worth a slower, more variable first token for voice regardless?*
- **Deprecated sampling parameters** (medium, since 2026-08-25) — Google's 3.7 Flash migration guidance says to remove `temperature`, `top_p` and `top_k` and calls them unsupported on Gemini 3.x; the API accepts and ignores them today (probed: 200 with `temperature=0.7`, `top_p=0.9` on gemini-3.7-flash) and is expected to reject them in later model generations. `_build_generation_params` still forwards them, so a user tuning temperature on a Gemini 3 model gets silence. — *Needs a call: a per-model warning like `_warn_if_thinking_budget_ignored`, or just a docstring deprecation on the Settings fields?*
- **Interactions API** (low, since 2026-08-25) — GA since 2026-06-22 and now Google's primary interface; `generateContent` remains supported for mainline models but Google points multi-turn conversations at server-side `previous_interaction_id`. — *Needs a call: whether Pipecat tracks the Interactions API at all, and when.*
- **`media_resolution` Settings field** (low, since 2026-08-25) — documented for Gemini 3 vision/document input and already exposed on `GeminiLiveLLMService`; on `GoogleLLMService` it is reachable only through `extra`. — *Needs a call: does a dedicated field earn its place when `extra` covers it, and does a top-level field suffice when Gemini 3 also takes per-part resolutions?*
- **Examples pin gemini-2.5-flash** (low, since 2026-08-25) — four `examples/voice/voice-google*.py` bots pass `model="gemini-2.5-flash"` while the service defaults to `gemini-3.6-flash`; 2.5 Flash has no announced shutdown date, so this is hygiene. — *Needs a call: should the examples track the service default, or is the older, cheaper model deliberate?*
