# Google LLM — 2026-11-23

`gemini-3.6-flash` stays the default and nothing here needs a branch: the Gemini 4 thinking defaults this service wants are already in flight as #6962, opened against the shared `_THINKING_DEFAULTS` table. The one dated item is 2026-12-01, when `gemini-2.5-flash` and `gemini-2.5-pro` start being served by Gemini 3 models while the service keeps sending them a `thinking_budget`.

## What's new for Pipecat

### PRs
- https://github.com/pipecat-ai/pipecat/pull/6962 — Send the Gemini 4 models the lowest thinking level they accept (opened this run by the `google/interactions-llm` unit; `_THINKING_DEFAULTS` is shared, so it closes this unit's Gemini 4 gap too)

### To consider
- **Gemini 2.5 thinking defaults after the 2026-12-01 reroute** (high, since 2026-11-09) — eight days out. `gemini-2.5-flash` and `gemini-2.5-pro` were deprecated on 2026-11-02 and are served by `gemini-3.5-flash-lite` and `gemini-3.8-flash` from 2026-12-01. `_THINKING_DEFAULTS` keys off the name the caller passes, so those requests keep sending `thinking_budget` 0 / 128 into the Gemini 3 family, which takes `thinking_level`; the service's own warning text says such a budget may be ignored or rejected. Removing the entries before the date would instead turn thinking on while the real 2.5 models are still answering. — *Needs a call: drop the entries on the reroute date, or map the retired names to the successor's `thinking_level`?*
- **gemini-3.8-flash as the default** (medium, since 2026-09-14) — unchanged in substance from last run and re-measured: aiewf-eval medium-context has `gemini-3.8-flash (low)` at 99.9% pass / 1411 ms TTFAT P50 against `gemini-3.6-flash (minimal)` at 95.1% / 808 ms, while my probes put 3.8 at 1287 ms vs 898 ms on a trivial prompt (median of 5) and 1397 ms vs 869 ms on a reasoning prompt (median of 3), `thinking_ms` 0 throughout. The gap narrowed from last run's ~60% to ~45%, still well outside the 10% band, and 3.8 Flash has no level below `low`. — *Needs a call: trade ~400 ms of TTFAT for the benchmark gain, or keep the faster default?*
- **Deprecated sampling parameters** (medium, since 2026-08-25) — Google's Gemini 3.8 Flash guide says `temperature`, `top_p` and `top_k` are ignored by the backend; `_build_generation_params` still forwards them, so tuning them on a Gemini 3 model is silent in both directions. — *Needs a call: a per-model warning like `_warn_if_thinking_budget_ignored`, or just a docstring deprecation on the Settings fields?*
- **Multimodal function responses** (low, since 2026-11-09) — a function response may carry audio, video, Office/OpenDocument content or a file URI, and google-genai 3.6.0 ships the types; Pipecat's function-call results are text/JSON only. — *Needs a call: does multimodal tool output belong in the universal `LLMContext` result shape first?*
- **`media_resolution` Settings field** (low, since 2026-08-25) — documented for Gemini 3 vision/document input and already exposed on `GeminiLiveLLMService`; here only `extra` reaches it. — *Needs a call: does a dedicated field earn its place when `extra` covers it, and does a top-level field suffice when Gemini 3 also takes per-part resolutions?*
- **Gemini 4 as a default candidate** (low, since 2026-11-23) — Carbon, Hydrogen and Neon all went stable this month and the models page tells new projects to use Hydrogen or 3.5 Flash-Lite, but aiewf-eval has benchmarked none of them, and Hydrogen at `thinking_level low` probes 1426 ms against the default's 898 ms. — *Needs a call: nothing yet; re-check when a Gemini 4 row appears on the leaderboard.*
- **ThinkingConfig levels are documented for Gemini 2.5 and 3 only** (low, since 2026-11-23) — Hydrogen and Neon add an `xhigh` level and Gemini 4 rejects `minimal`; the docstring says nothing about the family and the `Literal` omits `xhigh`, though `| str` keeps it usable. — *Needs a call: enumerate every model's levels in the docstring, or keep it to families and link Google's model pages?*

Closed since last run: the image example's `gemini-2.5-flash-image` pin, fixed by pipecat-ai/pipecat#6641 (merged 2026-11-09); the `google-genai` pin gap, closed by `Move the google extra to google-genai 3` — `pyproject.toml` now asks for `>=3.2.0,<4` and the latest release, 3.6.1, is inside it. Checked and not a gap: `gemini-3.7-flash`, which now tops the Flash rows on aiewf-eval (99.9% / 821 ms P50), is deprecated and auto-routes to 3.8, so it is not a bump candidate; no Google LLM example pins a retired model; `_PREFILL_SUPPORTED_PATTERNS` correctly treats the Gemini 4 names as not supporting a trailing model turn; tool retrieval (preview, 2026-11-19) and the Decisions API are new surfaces this service does not call.
