# Crusoe LLM — 2026-10-28

Crusoe changed nothing this cycle; aiewf-eval did — it rejudged the board and archived the row that stood in for our default, so the default-model call now needs a maintainer's judgement about the benchmark itself, not just about which model to pick.

## What's new for Pipecat

### PRs
Nothing.

### To consider
- **Default model quality** (high, since 2026-09-14) — on 2026-10-17 aiewf-eval switched judges (Opus 5.5, frozen text rubric), rejudged every row, and archived `gpt-oss-120b (groq)` at the maintainer's request; the archive says its 86.3% was copied over "without rescoring or recalculating latency". The default therefore has no score under the judge that produced every candidate's number, which is exactly the comparison #5447 asks for. Under the new judge the Crusoe-hosted field is led by `deepseek-v4-flash-0731 (high)` 98.6% / 773 ms and `deepseek-v4-pro-0813 (low)` 98.4% / 755 ms — and Crusoe's model page links its `deepseek-ai/DeepSeek-V4-Flash` and `deepseek-ai/DeepSeek-V4-Pro` entries to precisely those two Hugging Face checkpoints, so for the first time a leaderboard row maps to a model Crusoe serves without inference. Last run's shortlist both fell: `glm-5.3 (low)` 98.7% → 97.7%, `qwen3.8-27b (thinking off, FP8)` 98.2% → 96.0%. Crusoe lists DeepSeek V4 Flash as the migration path for three retired models, the closest thing it publishes to a recommendation, but it does not call it gpt-oss-120b's successor and gpt-oss-120b carries no deprecation notice, so this stays a quality call rather than a forced migration. — *Needs a call: whether to take DeepSeek V4 Pro or V4 Flash, and whether an archived pre-rejudge 86.3% counts as "the current default's pass rate" for #5447's purposes; either way a working `CRUSOE_API_KEY` is still required before a bump can be probed.*
- **Unprobeable unit** (medium, since 2026-09-01) — the CI credential has now been rejected with HTTP 401 on every endpoint for four consecutive runs. This blocks the item above, not merely its verification. — *Needs a call: who reissues the Crusoe key, or whether the unit should be marked docs-only until one exists.*
