### To consider
- **Registry still scaffolds the legacy endpoint** (medium, since 2026-08-31) — Sarvam's streaming-api page calls the endpoint `SarvamSTTService` uses "the generally-available legacy WebSocket" and states that realtime supersedes it for new voice-agent and live-transcription work, with no sunset date and realtime still labelled beta there. `sarvam_stt` in `cli/registry/service_metadata.py` still lists `class_name=["SarvamSTTService"]`, and the unit now carries three classes after `SarvamHttpSTTService` landed. — *Needs a call: whether SarvamRealtimeSTTService, or the new HTTP class, should join the registry's Sarvam STT entry while Sarvam still calls realtime beta.*
- **sarvamai pin behind PyPI** (low, since 2026-08-31) — `pyproject.toml` pins `sarvamai==0.1.28`; 0.1.45 shipped 2026-11-09. Against the pinned wheel, 0.1.45 adds `keyterms` and `with_timestamps` to `speech_to_text.transcribe()`, `keyterms` to `speech_to_text_streaming.connect()`, and the whole `speech_to_text_realtime_streaming` module. Our classes reach `keyterms` by hand through `request_options`, so nothing is out of reach. — *Needs a call: whether the exact pin moves now that the parameters we hand-roll are first-class upstream.*
- **SDK ships a realtime client** (low, since 2026-09-14) — `sarvamai.speech_to_text_realtime_streaming` covers the endpoint `SarvamRealtimeSTTService` implements by hand, and its 0.1.45 connect signature matches `_query_params()` field for field. — *Needs a call: whether the hand-rolled realtime websocket is worth replacing with the SDK client, which would also require moving the pin.*
- **REST `with_timestamps` unreachable** (low, since 2026-11-09) — Sarvam's REST reference documents `with_timestamps`, returning chunk-level (not word-level) offsets; `SarvamHttpSTTSettings` is empty and `_transcribe()` never sends it, where the realtime class does expose the equivalent `return_timestamps`. — *Needs a call: whether chunk offsets are meaningful when a segment over 30 seconds is split into pieces whose offsets each restart at zero.*
