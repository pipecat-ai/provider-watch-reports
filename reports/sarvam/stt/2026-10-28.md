### To consider

The one STT-facing change across the range is still the `keyterms` parameter on `speech_to_text_streaming.connect()`, which our service already sends through `request_options.additional_query_parameters`. — *Needs a call: whether the exact pin moves when the only STT-facing change is a parameter we already pass by hand.*
