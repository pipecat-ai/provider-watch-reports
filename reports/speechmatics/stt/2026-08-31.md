# Speechmatics STT — 2026-08-31

`split_sentences` crashes the service at construction; the branch fixes that and adds an `include_results` Settings field. Otherwise the service tracks the provider, but `operating_point` is on its way out.

## What's new for Pipecat

### PRs
- `provider-watch/speechmatics-stt-include-results` — review: `git show provider-watch/speechmatics-stt-include-results` — Fix the split_sentences crash and expose Speechmatics word-level results

### To consider
- **`operating_point` → `model`** (medium, since 2026-08-31) — Speechmatics' Models page deprecates `operating_point` in SaaS in favour of `model`, and the installed SDK logs `TranscriptionConfig.operating_point is deprecated ... Please use the model property instead.` on every session. speechmatics-voice 0.3.0b8 replaces the field with `model: Model`, which will break `default_settings.model = self._config.operating_point.value` and `Settings.operating_point`. — *Needs a call: whether to wait for 0.3.0 GA (0.2.8 is still the latest stable and matches our `~=0.2.8` pin) or alias `model` onto the 0.2.x field now.*
- **Global realtime endpoint** (medium, since 2026-08-31) — the documented Realtime SaaS endpoints are `global.rt.speechmatics.com`, `eu.rt.speechmatics.com` and `us.rt.speechmatics.com`; the service defaults to `wss://eu2.rt.speechmatics.com/v2`, an undocumented alias of the EU endpoint. A probe against the global endpoint transcribed fine. — *Needs a call: the global endpoint may route to any region, so changing the default needs a stance on data residency.*
- **`speech_segment_config` is clobbered** (medium, since 2026-08-31) — `_build_config` assigns a fresh `SpeechSegmentConfig(emit_sentences=...)` after the `extra_params` loop, so `add_trailing_eos` and `pause_mark` cannot be reached by any route. — *Needs a call: setting `emit_sentences` in place instead would let the SDK preset default (True) through, changing behaviour for existing users.*
- **Turn-detection sub-configs** (medium, since 2026-08-31) — `vad_config`, `smart_turn_config` and `end_of_turn_config` tune the very modes `TurnDetectionMode` selects, but are only settable via `extra_params`. — *Needs a call: they are nested SDK models, so exposing them needs a stance on whether Settings accepts SDK types directly.*
- **Language and locale maps** (low, since 2026-08-31) — `BASE_LANGUAGES` omits `tl`, `ia` and the five bilingual packs (each still works, with a "Language ... not verified" warning); `LOCALES` covers only the three English locales although the docs also document Mandarin and Portuguese output locales. — *Needs a call: bilingual pack codes have no `Language` enum member.*
- **Remaining config options and presets** (low, since 2026-08-31) — `enable_entities`, `max_delay_mode`, `transcription_update_preset`, `audio_buffer_length` and `advanced_engine_control` are reachable only through `extra_params`, and `TurnDetectionMode` exposes four of the SDK's seven presets (`fast`, `captions`, `scribe` are absent). — *Needs a call: whether `extra_params` is considered sufficient here.*
