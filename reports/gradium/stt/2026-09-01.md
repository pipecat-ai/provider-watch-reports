# Gradium STT — 2026-09-01

`default` is still the model to ship, but Gradium's 300 s session cap is the item
to look at; nothing could be probed because `GRADIUM_API_KEY` is not set in this
environment.

## What's new for Pipecat

### PRs
Nothing.

### To consider
- **300 s session cap** (high, since 2026-09-01) — Gradium's Limits page caps one STT session at 300 seconds, but `GradiumSTTService` connects once in `setup()` and holds the socket for the whole call. If Gradium ends the session with a normal close, `WebsocketService._receive_task_handler` breaks on `ConnectionClosedOK` without reconnecting, so transcription would go quiet past five minutes with no error. — *Needs a call: a >300 s probe to learn the close code, then whether to rotate the socket proactively or reconnect on the close.*
- **Keyword boosting** (medium, since 2026-09-01) — `json_config.keywords` (`{"words": [...], "boost": 3}`, up to 500 single-token entries, live since 2026-07-22) is not reachable; a `keywords` Settings field passed into the setup message would cover it. — *Needs a call: the field shape (raw dict vs typed model) and whether `keyword_boost_id` for Studio-saved dictionaries comes along; probe blocked on GRADIUM_API_KEY.*
- **Translating model** (medium, since 2026-09-01) — `model="stt-translate"` transcribes into another language chosen with `target_language`, which Settings has no field for, and our `language` handling assumes it names the audio's language. — *Needs a call: whether translated transcription belongs in an STT service, given the emitted `TranscriptionFrame` would carry the output language, not the audio's.*
- **PCM rate fallback** (medium, since 2026-09-01) — `_input_format_from_encoding` maps only 8000/16000/24000 and warns-then-uses `pcm_16000` for anything else; Gradium accepts `pcm_22050`, `pcm_44100` and `pcm_48000`, so a 48 kHz pipeline gets mis-transcribed rather than rejected. — *Needs a call: none in principle, but the fix needs a 48 kHz probe, blocked on GRADIUM_API_KEY.*
- **Required language** (medium, since 2026-09-01) — Gradium made `json_config.language` mandatory on 2026-08-24; `Settings(language=None)` omits it and the setup is rejected. — *Needs a call: fall back to `"any"` or reject at construction time.*
- **`temp` and `padding_bonus`** (low, since 2026-09-01) — documented tuning knobs (`temp` 0.0–1.5, `padding_bonus` -4.0–4.0 to bias text earlier or later) with no Settings fields. — *Needs a call: none beyond probe verification, blocked on GRADIUM_API_KEY.*
- **Docstring and flush drift** (low, since 2026-09-01) — the Settings docstring pins `delay_in_frames` to ten discrete values; Gradium documents any int 0–80, default 10, while we default to 12. `_send_flush` also sends `flush_id` as a string where the reference types it as an integer. — *Needs a call: whether to move our default to Gradium's 10, and confirmation that a string flush_id stays accepted.*
- **Semantic VAD `step`** (low, since 2026-09-01) — Gradium streams `step` messages every 80 ms carrying per-horizon `inactivity_prob`, which its turn-taking recipe uses for endpointing; the service drops them and flushes on Pipecat's own VAD instead. — *Needs a call: whether a provider-side turn signal belongs in the STT service or in a turn strategy.*
