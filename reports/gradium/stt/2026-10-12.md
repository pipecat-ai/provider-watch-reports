# Gradium STT — 2026-10-12

Nothing to act on urgently: `default` remains the model to ship, Gradium shipped
no STT changes in October, and the two items last run ranked highest were fixed
in-tree on 2026-10-04. What is left is five Settings-coverage items, four of
them unchanged since 2026-09-01. Nothing could be probed — `GRADIUM_API_KEY` is
not set in this environment.

## What's new for Pipecat

### PRs
Nothing.

### To consider
- **Keyword boosting** (medium, since 2026-09-01) — `json_config.keywords` is not reachable from `Settings`; a `keywords` field passed into the setup message would cover it. Gradium now has a dedicated recipe for it: `{"words": [...], "boost": 3}`, boost valid from -6 to 6 and applied in log-probability space, 3 recommended, 1–2 a gentle nudge, 5–6 risking the decoder repeating a keyword, negative values de-boosting. Up to 500 entries, each case/accent variant counting separately, multi-word names split into space-free tokens. Most useful at low `delay_in_frames`. Still no saved-dictionary or `keyword_boost_id` equivalent, so the words go inline on every connection. — *Needs a call: the field shape, raw dict vs a typed model carrying the documented boost range; probe blocked on GRADIUM_API_KEY.*
- **Translating model** (medium, since 2026-09-01) — `model="stt-translate"` transcribes into another language chosen with `target_language`, which `Settings` has no field for, and our `language` handling assumes it names the audio's language. Translation output is en/fr/de/es/pt only (no `any`). — *Needs a call: whether translated transcription belongs in an STT service, given the emitted `TranscriptionFrame` would carry the output language, not the audio's.*
- **Required language** (medium, since 2026-09-01) — `json_config.language` is mandatory ("Requests without it are rejected"); `Settings(language=None)` omits it and the setup is rejected. — *Needs a call: fall back to `"any"` or reject at construction time.*
- **`temp` and `padding_bonus`** (low, since 2026-09-01) — documented tuning knobs with no `Settings` fields (`padding_bonus` -4.0 to 4.0 to bias text earlier or later; `temp` listed as 0.0–1.5 on the settings guide, and as up to 1.0 defaulting to 0 on the endpoint reference). — *Needs a call: none beyond probe verification (blocked on GRADIUM_API_KEY) and picking which of the two documented `temp` ranges holds.*
- **Delay default, allowed values and flush_id type** (low, since 2026-09-01) — we default `delay_in_frames` to 12 where Gradium documents 10. Three provider pages now disagree on the allowed values: our docstring's ten match the endpoint reference, the turn-taking recipe lists eleven (adding 32), and the settings guide describes any int 0–80. `_send_flush` sends `flush_id` as a string where the reference types it an integer. — *Needs a call: whether to move our default to Gradium's 10, and confirmation that a string flush_id stays accepted.*

Two items from 2026-09-14 are gone, both fixed in-tree rather than by the
provider. **Stale state after a reconnect** was the high-priority item: a8c57d1fa
(2026-10-04) added a `_reconnect_websocket` override that finalizes the text
received so far and calls `_reset_connection_state()`, keeping an open turn open
across the new connection, with tests at `tests/test_gradium_stt.py:338` and
`:360`. **PCM rate fallback** is resolved by 5b9947028 (2026-10-04):
`_gradium_pcm_sample_rate` now resamples input to the lowest rate Gradium
accepts at or above the pipeline's, or 24 kHz above that, instead of relabelling
48 kHz audio as `pcm_16000`. Gradium's own recommended PCM input is 24 kHz, so
not sending the native `pcm_44100`/`pcm_48000` formats costs only a resample and
is not worth carrying as a gap.
