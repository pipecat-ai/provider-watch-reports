# Camb TTS — 2026-08-31

`mars-flash` is still GA and correctly the default; the credential is dead, so every finding below is documentation-only and no branch was left.

## What's new for Pipecat

### PRs
Nothing.

### To consider
- **MARS 8.1 beta sample rates** (medium, since 2026-08-31) — `mars-8.1-flash-beta` and `mars-8.1-pro-beta` ship in the SDK's `speech_model` enum (camb-sdk 1.5.17) and are the streaming endpoint's documented default, but `MODEL_SAMPLE_RATES` knows only the three GA models, so both betas fall back to 22050 Hz against Camb's documented 48 kHz — audio labelled at less than half its real rate. Adding two entries would do it. Camb also lists `mars-flash` as "22.05kHz/48kHz", which one table entry cannot express. — *Needs a call: a live probe must confirm the betas' actual stream rate (blocked), and whether beta models belong in the table at all.*
- **`voice_settings` pass-through** (medium, since 2026-08-31) — the streaming TTS endpoint takes `voice_settings` with `speaking_rate` (default 1), `maintain_source_accent` and `enhance_reference_audio_quality`; `run_tts` never builds the object, so there is no speed control. — *Needs a call: whether to expose `speaking_rate` alone or the whole nested object, and a probe with it set (blocked).*
- **Requested sample rate never reaches Camb** (medium, since 2026-08-31) — `output_configuration` has a documented `sample_rate` field; the service sets only `format="pcm_s16le"` and, when the caller passes `sample_rate=`, logs a warning while still tagging frames at the caller's rate over a stream at the model's native rate. — *Needs a call: request the rate from Camb, or keep warning-only.*
- **`inference_options` pass-through** (low, since 2026-08-31) — `stability`, `temperature`, `inference_steps`, `speaker_similarity` and friends are unreachable; documented as MARS-8-family-only and unsupported on the two 8.1 betas. — *Needs a call: what to do when the selected model ignores them.*
- **`enhance_named_entities_pronunciation`** (low, since 2026-08-31) — top-level boolean, default false, not exposed. — *Needs a call: a probe with the field set, which the rejected credential blocks.*
