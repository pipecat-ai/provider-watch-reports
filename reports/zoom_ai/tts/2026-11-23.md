# Zoom AI TTS — 2026-11-23

Current: the Fast-mode schema is small (`input`, `voice`, `speed`, `volume`, `response_format`) and
`ZoomAITTSSettings` reaches all of it; nothing is retired and no code change is warranted.

## What's new for Pipecat

### PRs

Nothing.

### To consider

- **Throttling is not retried** (low, since 2026-11-23) — Zoom's Fast-mode error table documents
  `Retry-After` on 429 `RATE_LIMIT_EXCEEDED` and on 503 `SERVICE_UNAVAILABLE` (plus
  `metadata.retry_after_seconds`) and recommends exponential backoff with jitter. `_synthesize`
  pushes an error and returns, so a throttled sentence is dropped from the bot's speech. —
  *Needs a call: whether retrying belongs in the service or in the application, and a key to probe
  the throttled path.*

Checked without a probe, against the Fast-mode reference: the endpoint
(`https://api.zoom.us/v2/aiservices/tts/synthesize`), `response_format: "pcm_44100"` as the only
supported format, `ZOOM_TTS_SAMPLE_RATE = 44100` (signed 16-bit LE mono, no container),
`MAX_INPUT_CHARS = 4000` code points with the 32 KiB body cap that `ensure_ascii=False` keeps the
request inside, the documented `speed` range 0.5–2.0 and `volume` range above 0 up to 10 matching
the `Settings` docstring, the required `Authorization` and `Content-Type` headers plus the
`x-zm-trackingid` the error messages quote, and the absence of `model` and `language` fields, which
the service correctly sets to `None`. Voices pass through unchecked, so all ten documented IDs work
and new ones need no Pipecat change; `jenna` (the default) and `ava` (the examples) are both still
listed.
