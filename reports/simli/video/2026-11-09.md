PR #6400 merged on 2026-10-30, so last run's three `pr` gaps are closed in the code; Simli
has released nothing since (simli-ai still 2.1.1, no new docs pages), and the five remaining
items are all design calls, so no branch this run.

## What's new for Pipecat

### PRs
Nothing.

### To consider
- **Trinity-2's live controls are not reachable** (medium, since 2026-10-28) — `SimliClient` in 2.1.1 carries `setCamera(camera, lerp_seconds=...)`, `setBackground(id)` and `setForeground(id)` (confirmed on the installed wheel), sending the `CAM_`/`BG_`/`FG_` messages that move the camera and swap assets while the session runs. #6400 added the *starting* framing only, so nothing can change once the session is up. — *Needs a call: whether a mid-session control becomes a frame, a method, or both; these are the first Simli controls that act during a session, and AGENTS.md prefers a frame.*
- **`enable_logging` does nothing** (medium, since 2026-08-31) — the constructor and the deprecated `InputParams` both take it, but it is never passed to `SimliConfig` or `SimliClient`. simli-ai 2.1.1 documents no logging option; the SDK logs through the stdlib logger named `simli_client`. — *Needs a call: deprecate the argument, or honour it by configuring that logger.*
- **Session events are not wired up** (medium, since 2026-09-01) — a session Simli ends or errors mid-call is silent: no `registerEventCallback` for START/STOP/ERROR/SILENT/SPEAK, `stopReason` and `error` unread, the two stream iterators simply end and the avatar freezes with nothing upstream. — *Needs a call: which events become Pipecat event handlers versus an internal `push_error` on ERROR/STOP, and whether SPEAK/SILENT add anything over the audio frames the service already pushes.*
- **`handleSilence` is not reachable** (low, since 2026-08-31) — documented on `SimliConfig` and in the `/compose/token` body (default `true`); off, the avatar freezes rather than idling when the buffer drains. — *Needs a call: whether it joins the constructor the way `framing=` did, both being fixed at session start.*
- **Transport and retry options are fixed** (low, since 2026-08-31) — `enableSFU=True` is hard-coded; `transport_mode` (P2P/LIVEKIT) and `retry_count` are not exposed. `retry_timeout` has stopped mattering: 2.1 deprecates and ignores it in favour of exponential backoff with jitter. — *Needs a call: whether the LiveKit transport is wanted at all from Pipecat, which already owns the WebRTC leg.*
- **Simli's docs still describe the pre-#6400 service** (low, since 2026-11-09) — their workarounds page has Pipecat users reach into `simli._simli_client.config.__dict__` for Trinity-2 framing, greet on connect to get video, and avoid GPT-Live; `SimliVideoService` now has `framing=`, shows video from connect, and consumes `SpeechOutputAudioRawFrame`. Their Pipecat page's constructor example still shows only `api_key`/`face_id`/`max_session_length`/`max_idle_time`. — *Needs a call: whether we ask Simli to update their pages or just document `framing=` on our side.*

### Resolved since the last run

The pin is now `simli-ai~=2.1.0`, `SimliVideoService.Framing`/`.Camera` exist, and `process_frame` handles both audio frame types.
