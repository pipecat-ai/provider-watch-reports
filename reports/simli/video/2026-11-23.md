## What's new for Pipecat

### PRs
Nothing.

### To consider
- **Trinity-2's live controls are not reachable** (medium, since 2026-10-28) — `SimliClient` 2.1.1 carries `setCamera(camera, lerp_seconds=...)`, `setBackground(id)` and `setForeground(id)` (confirmed on the installed wheel and in Simli's Python reference), sending the `CAM_`/`BG_`/`FG_` messages. The service sends the *starting* framing only, while Simli's Trinity-2 page sells the camera and the assets as things you "change live". — *Needs a call: whether a mid-session control becomes a frame, a method, or both; these are the first Simli controls that act during a session, and AGENTS.md prefers a frame.*
- **`enable_logging` does nothing** (medium, since 2026-08-31) — the constructor and the deprecated `InputParams` both take it, but it is never passed to `SimliConfig` or `SimliClient`. simli-ai 2.1.1 documents no logging option; the SDK logs through the stdlib logger named `simli_client`. — *Needs a call: deprecate the argument, or honour it by configuring that logger.*
- **Session events are not wired up** (medium, since 2026-09-01) — a session Simli ends or errors mid-call is silent: no `registerEventCallback` for START/STOP/ERROR/SILENT/SPEAK, `stopReason` and `error` unread, the two stream iterators simply end and the avatar freezes with nothing upstream. — *Needs a call: which events become Pipecat event handlers versus an internal `push_error` on ERROR/STOP, and whether SPEAK/SILENT add anything over the audio frames the service already pushes.*
- **`handleSilence` is not reachable** (low, since 2026-08-31) — documented on `SimliConfig` and in the `/compose/token` body (default `true`); off, the avatar freezes rather than idling when the buffer drains. `SimliVideoSettings` is empty and the service reads no settings, so this is a constructor argument, not a pass-through `Settings` field. — *Needs a call: whether it joins the constructor the way `framing=` did, both being fixed at session start.*
- **Transport and retry options are fixed** (low, since 2026-08-31) — `enableSFU=True` is hard-coded; `transport_mode` (P2P/LIVEKIT) and `retry_count` are not exposed. `retry_timeout` is deprecated and ignored by 2.1 in favour of exponential backoff with jitter. — *Needs a call: whether the LiveKit transport is wanted at all from Pipecat, which already owns the WebRTC leg.*

### Resolved since the last run
