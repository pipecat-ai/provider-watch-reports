# Deepgram Flux STT — 2026-09-01

Nothing changed on the provider side since the last run: `flux-general-en` remains the default, PR 5523 carries the only PR-able gap, and Deepgram's 2026-08-28 turn-control release is still the one item needing a decision.

## What's new for Pipecat

### PRs

- https://github.com/pipecat-ai/pipecat/pull/5523 — Expose Flux's profanity_filter and redact settings (open)

### To consider

- **`ForceEndTurn` has no entry point** (medium, since 2026-09-01) — Deepgram's manual turn control: `{"type": "ForceEndTurn"}` ends the active turn immediately, `eot_threshold=1.0` suppresses native detection entirely, and the two combine into "bring your own turn detection", switchable mid-stream via `Configure`. Pipecat can already set `eot_threshold` (including to 1.0) and both transports have `_transport_send_json`, but nothing sends the message, so the mode is unreachable. `EndOfTurn` now also carries `trigger` (`model` / `manual` / `timeout`), reachable only by digging into `TranscriptionFrame.result`. — *Needs a call: whether this becomes a public service method, a new frame, or an integration with the user-turn strategies — that choice decides how an external turn detector drives Flux.*
- **Stale constructor docstrings** (low, since 2026-09-01) — `DeepgramFluxSTTService.__init__` documents `url` as "the preview endpoint" although `wss://api.deepgram.com/v2/listen` is Flux's GA endpoint, and both `flux_encoding` docstrings say the encoding "Must be `linear16`" while Flux documents linear16, linear32, mulaw, alaw, opus and ogg-opus. — *Needs a call: whether Flux should accept the other encodings at all, given Pipecat transports always hand the service linear16 PCM.*

`DeepgramFluxSageMakerSTTService` was not probed: it needs a deployed SageMaker endpoint. It shares `DeepgramFluxSTTBase._build_query_string`, so PR 5523 covers it too. Deepgram's model catalogue endpoint still omits Flux entirely, so `models_seen` comes from the docs. Deepgram's September announcements are about Flux **TTS** (GA, free through 2026-09-12), a different unit.
