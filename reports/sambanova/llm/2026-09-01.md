# SambaNova LLM — 2026-09-01

`Meta-Llama-3.3-70B-Instruct` is current and not deprecated; the one fix is that the service silently drops three sampling parameters SambaNova accepts.

## What's new for Pipecat

### PRs
- https://github.com/pipecat-ai/pipecat/pull/5565 — Send SambaNova's frequency_penalty, presence_penalty and seed

### To consider
- **Default model age** (low, since 2026-09-01) — `Meta-Llama-3.3-70B-Instruct` is the oldest of SambaNova's four production models (the others are `MiniMax-M2.7`, `DeepSeek-V3.1`, `gpt-oss-120b`), but SambaNova lists it as a *replacement* target, not a deprecated one. — *Needs a call: SambaNova designates no successor, and aiewf-eval's medium-context leaderboard has no row for any Llama model or any SambaNova-hosted route, so there is no comparable benchmark to justify a bump.*
- **openai SDK pin** (low, since 2026-09-01) — our pin `openai>=1.74.0,<3` stops short of openai 3.6.0 (2026-08-28); nothing in SambaNova's schema needs 3.x. — *Needs a call: the pin is shared by every OpenAI-compatible service and belongs to the openai unit.*
