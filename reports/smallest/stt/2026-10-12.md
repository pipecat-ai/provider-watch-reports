# Smallest STT — 2026-10-12

Smallest shipped `pulse-2` since the last run — English-only streaming with built-in end-of-turn
detection plus emotion and gender on finals. It works through `SmallestSTTService` unchanged (probed),
and open PR #6018 already adds selection and the two new switches, so nothing to branch. What is not
covered anywhere: the service's pulse-tuned finalization (`endpointing` on every connection, a client
`finalize` per turn, and `eou_timeout_ms` from the other open PR) is exactly what pulse-2 tells clients
not to send. `pulse` stays the right default: it is not deprecated, and pulse-2 is positioned as the
English-only sibling, not the successor.

## What's new for Pipecat

### PRs
- https://github.com/pipecat-ai/pipecat/pull/6018 — Add Pulse 2.0 model support to Smallest AI STT service (open since 2026-10-02; adds `SmallestSTTModel.PULSE_2`, `emotion_detection` / `gender_detection` settings, and an English-only guard)
- https://github.com/pipecat-ai/pipecat/pull/5518 — Add eou_timeout_ms and itn_normalize to SmallestSTTService settings (open since 2026-08-31)

### To consider
- **Language map vs. Pulse's streaming set** (high, since 2026-08-31) — 15 mapped codes are pre-recorded-only or undocumented on streaming; `language=pl` fails the WebSocket handshake and the user sees only a reconnection error. The streaming East Asian codes (`zh`, `yue`, `ja`, `ko`) and the three aggregators are missing from the map, and the East Asian four additionally require `wss://api.us.smallest.ai` rather than the default `base_url`. The card now also lists `hi-dev` (Devanagari-only Hindi, India host), which has no `Language` member. — *Needs a call: trimming the map breaks anyone passing those codes today, so choose between removing them, failing with a clear message, or documenting the streaming/batch and region split.*
- **Pulse-tuned finalization reaches pulse-2** (medium, since 2026-10-12) — the service always sends `endpointing` and a client `finalize` on every `VADUserStoppedSpeakingFrame`; pulse-2's card says its finalization params "are ignored by default - don't send them. If you do, they still take effect and compete with end-of-turn detection", and PR #5518 would add one of them, `eou_timeout_ms`, to `Settings`. The card and the response-format page also disagree on whether a `finalize`-flushed final sets `is_last`; if the card is right, `_process_response` logs "server will close session" every turn. A one-turn probe on pulse-2 succeeded, so this is a sharp edge rather than a break. — *Needs a call: whether the service should suppress its pulse-tuned finalization when the model is pulse-2, and confirmation from Smallest on the `is_last` contradiction.*
- **Finalize control** (medium, since 2026-08-31) — `finalize_on_words` and `max_words` are absent from `Settings`; Pulse documents `finalize_on_words=false` plus a per-turn client `finalize` as the agentic pattern, which the service already does, while pulse-2 tells clients to stop sending both. — *Needs a call: they change when finals arrive, so it is a question of whether the service should adopt the agentic pattern by default rather than only pass the flags through.*
- **VAD events** (low, since 2026-08-31) — `vad_events=true` interleaves `speech_started` / `speech_ended` messages on the same socket for both models; `_process_response` reads only `transcript`, so they would be dropped. — *Needs a call: whether server VAD events should become frames at all, given the pipeline runs its own VAD.*
- **`numerals` is undocumented** (low, since 2026-08-31) — the service always sends `numerals=auto`, but neither the AsyncAPI spec nor pulse-2's parameter table has such a parameter, and both point at `itn_normalize` for written-form numbers. — *Needs a call: confirm with Smallest whether `numerals` is still honored before dropping the field.*
- **`punctuate` / `capitalize`** (low, since 2026-08-31) — the spec documents both alongside `format`, which is all `Settings` exposes and all the feature pages describe. — *Needs a call: whether the finer-grained pair earns two fields next to `format`.*
- **`external_session_id`** (low, since 2026-10-12) — documented on the pulse-2 card as a client-supplied id for correlating a session in your own logs; not reachable today. — *Needs a call: it is a per-session logging knob rather than a transcription setting, so decide whether it belongs in `Settings` or as a constructor argument.*
- **Pulse Pro** (low, since 2026-08-31) — GA, English-only, batch-only; the streaming worker is on Smallest's roadmap but not deployed, so using it needs a class against `POST /waves/v1/stt/`. — *Needs a call: a new service class is outside provider-watch's remit; decide whether a batch Smallest STT service is wanted.*
