# Cerebras LLM — 2026-09-01

Nothing to fix: `gpt-oss-120b` is GA, is the flagship on Cerebras's public catalogue, and works through `CerebrasLLMService`. The only open question is whether `gemma-4-31b` would be the better default for voice.

## What's new for Pipecat

### PRs
Nothing.

### To consider
- **gemma-4-31b as the default** (medium, since 2026-09-01) — aiewf-eval's medium-context leaderboard scores `gemma-4-31b-it` (thinking off) at 96.6% pass / 489 ms TTFAT P50 against `gpt-oss-120b` at 86.3% / 98 ms, but neither row was measured on Cerebras. On Cerebras the two are close in latency (medians of 5: 209 ms vs 238 ms), and `gpt-oss-120b` reasons on every turn, which widens its tail (210–638 ms). — *Needs a call: Cerebras does not position `gemma-4-31b` as a successor — it is a smaller, multimodal model in a different family — so changing the default rests on the off-provider leaderboard rows.*
