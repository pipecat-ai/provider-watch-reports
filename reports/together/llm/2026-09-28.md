# Together LLM — 2026-09-28

`zai-org/GLM-5.2` is still Together's current, non-deprecated serverless GLM and stays the default;
the branch makes Together's own `reasoning` toggle settable, and the maintainer's call is whether
it should be off by default.

## What's new for Pipecat

### PRs
- https://github.com/pipecat-ai/pipecat/pull/5939 — Expose Together's reasoning toggle through TogetherLLMSettings

### To consider
- **Reasoning on by default** (high, since 2026-09-01) — Together's chat-completions reference now
  documents a model-agnostic `reasoning` object, and the branch above makes it settable. What it
  does not decide is the default: out of the box `zai-org/GLM-5.2` reasoned for 8770–27475 ms
  before its first answer token on a plain scheduling request, with one of three probe turns
  exceeding the 30 s timeout — a dead line in a voice pipeline. The same prompt with
  `reasoning={"enabled": false}` answered in 239–615 ms, 3/3 clean. `reasoning_effort: "low"` is
  accepted but does not help (15562 ms of reasoning). — *Needs a call: whether
  `TogetherLLMService` should default `reasoning` to off, as `DeepSeekLLMService` does for its V4
  models and as #5446 did for Sonnet 5 and later.*
- **GLM-5.3 as the default** (low, since 2026-09-01) — GLM-5.3 is now well ahead of GLM-5.2 on a
  trivial prompt (TTFAT 344 ms against 1515 ms, medians of 5, all turns clean) but behind it on a
  reasoning-triggering one, where only one of three turns answered inside 30 s. aiewf-eval's
  `aiwf_medium_context` is unchanged: glm-5.3 (low reasoning) 98.7% / 2376 ms against glm-5.2
  (none) 99.7% / 936 ms, and there is still no GLM-5.3-Flash row. — *Needs a call: a released
  model's row is final, so GLM-5.3 only becomes the Together default if a maintainer overrides the
  benchmark condition (#5447) or the leaderboard gains a better row.*
- **Together's sampling extensions** (low, since 2026-09-28) — `top_k`, `min_p` and
  `repetition_penalty` are documented on the endpoint but are not OpenAI SDK keyword arguments, so
  `BaseOpenAILLMService`'s `params.update(self._settings.extra)` splat rejects them and users must
  nest them under `extra={"extra_body": {...}}`. The branch's `reasoning` field is the shape they
  would follow. — *Needs a call: whether these knobs are worth first-class fields for voice
  pipelines, or the `extra_body` idiom is enough.*

The previous report's `chat_template_kwargs` idiom still works but is no longer the thing to
document: it is GLM-template-specific, whereas `reasoning` is Together's own documented toggle and
covers every model that has one.
