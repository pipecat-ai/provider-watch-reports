### To consider
- **PAL/Face rename** (medium, since 2026-08-31) — the create-conversation reference now lists only `pal_id`/`face_id`, and Tavus's Pipecat integration page passes `face_id=os.getenv("TAVUS_FACE_ID")`, which our constructor rejects. The legacy names are undocumented but still accepted (probed today), so this is a naming/docs divergence rather than breakage. — *Needs a call: adding a `face_id`/`pal_id` alias means a new constructor parameter and a registry env-var change, plus a stance on deprecating `replica_id`.*
- **Conversation properties** (medium, since 2026-08-31) — `create_conversation` sends only `replica_id` and `persona_id`, so users cannot set `properties.max_call_duration` (3600 s cap), `participant_absent_timeout`, `apply_greenscreen` (useful when compositing the avatar into the bot's own video out), or the recording fields. — *Needs a call: which knobs to expose and where, since the conversation is created once in `setup()` from constructor args and the value must thread through three layers.*
