# Groq STT — 2026-11-23

Nothing moved on Groq's side: same two Whisper models, same request schema, no STT deprecation, and `groq/stt.py` is unchanged since the last run. Three gaps carry forward; a repo-side change adds a fourth, low-priority one.

## What's new for Pipecat

### PRs
Nothing.

### To consider
- **`language=None` errors instead of auto-detecting** (medium, since 2026-09-01) — `GroqSTTService._transcribe` still opens with `assert self._settings.language is not None`; today's probe with `language=None` yielded zero `TranscriptionFrame`s and one `ErrorFrame` reading `Unknown error occurred: ` (the AssertionError carries no message). Groq's speech-to-text reference still lists `language` as optional and Whisper detects the language when it is absent, so auto-detection is a capability the service cannot express. Dropping the assert and omitting the key when it is None is the shape of the fix. — *Needs a call: `openai/stt.py` has the identical assert, so this is either a Groq-only fix or a whisper-family one, and the gpt-4o transcribe models OpenAISTTService defaults to may not tolerate an omitted language the way Whisper does.*
- **`Settings.extra` never reaches the request** (medium, since 2026-09-01) — the kwargs dict in `_transcribe` covers `file`, `model`, `response_format`, `language`, `prompt` and `temperature` and nothing else, while `ServiceSettings.extra` exists on every settings object. Groq's `timestamp_granularities[]`, an explicit `response_format` and the `url` upload form have no path through the service; `response_format` is chosen solely by `include_prob_metrics`. The file has not changed since the 2026-11-09 probe that demonstrated the drop, so no new probe this run. — *Needs a call: whether `extra` should be spread into the whisper services' transcription requests at all, and whether word timestamps are worth exposing before `TranscriptionFrame` can carry them.*
- **10-second minimum billed length** (low, since 2026-09-01) — Groq bills every transcription request as at least 10 seconds, and `SegmentedSTTService` issues one request per utterance, so a bot with 1–3 s turns pays multiples of its audio duration. Nothing in the service docstring or the docs page says so. — *Needs a call: the note belongs in the docs repo; confirm the docs team wants it.*
- **Segments uploaded above 16 kHz** (low, since 2026-11-23) — `SegmentedSTTService.segment_sample_rate` (added 2026-11-10) lets a service receive its segments at a fixed rate, and the local models override it to 16 kHz; the whisper HTTP services keep the pipeline's rate, so a 24 kHz bot ships 1.5x the bytes Groq will actually read after its own downsample to 16 kHz mono. — *Needs a call: whether the smaller upload measurably lowers TTFB for 1–3 s segments, and whether the override belongs on `BaseWhisperSTTService` (OpenAI included) or on Groq alone.*

No default bump: the catalogue still holds exactly `whisper-large-v3-turbo` and `whisper-large-v3`, and Groq positions v3 as the accuracy pick at 2.8x the hourly price, not as a successor.
