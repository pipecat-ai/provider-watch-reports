### To consider
- **Real-time translation** (medium, since 2026-08-25) — v5 does transcription and translation on one stream via a `translation` config object (`one_way` with `target_language`, or `two_way` with `language_a`/`language_b`); `SonioxSTTSettings` has no field for it and `_connect_websocket` never sends the key. It is the only key in the WebSocket config reference the service does not send. — *Needs a call: translation tokens arrive interleaved with the originals and are distinguished only by `translation_status`, with no timestamps, so `_receive_messages` would have to decide whether they become their own frames or are dropped from the transcript — not a pass-through field.*

Last run's context-docstring gap is closed: pipecat-ai/pipecat#5566 merged on 2026-09-02, and `SonioxContextObject` now describes context by its two accepted forms rather than anchoring `context_version 2` to the retired `stt-rt-v3-preview`.
