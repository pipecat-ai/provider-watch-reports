# Mistral STT — 2026-11-09

The shipped default is now a deprecated model with a 2027-04-30 retirement, and its named successor is Public Preview and measurably slower — a decision is needed on timing, not a bump today.

## What's new for Pipecat

### PRs
Nothing.

### To consider
- **Default model deprecated** (high, since 2026-11-09) — `voxtral-mini-transcribe-realtime-2602` was deprecated on 2026-10-31 and retires 2027-04-30; Mistral's alternative is `voxtral-mini-transcribe-realtime-3`, released 2026-10-20. It transcribes correctly through `MistralSTTService` as-is, but it is Public Preview and its TTFB was 2955 ms against the current default's 1310 ms (medians of 5), and forcing `target_streaming_delay_ms=240` left it at 2993 ms. A bump would also need `MISTRAL_TTFS_P99` (1.89 s) re-measured. — *Needs a call: wait for realtime 3 to reach GA and get faster, or bump early and accept the latency, given the retirement date.*
- **SDK pin behind Mistral's current major** (medium, since 2026-11-09) — our extra pins `mistralai>=2.0.0,<3`; 3.7.0 shipped 2026-11-01 and its realtime transcription session carries `language`, `context_bias`, `diarize`, `speaker_references` and `turn_detection`, none of which exist in the 2.x session model. Every symbol `stt.py` imports and the whole `RealtimeConnection` surface it uses are unchanged in 3.7.0. — *Needs a call: bumping a provider SDK major also moves `MistralTTSService` and the lockfile, so it is not an STT-only decision.*
- **Provider-side turn detection unused** (medium, since 2026-11-09) — realtime 3 scores end-of-turn while transcribing and emits `turn.start`, `turn.eager_end`, `turn.resume` and `turn.end` (with `end_reason`), configured by a `turn_detection` session object that must be sent before the first audio chunk. The service flushes audio on `VADUserStoppedSpeakingFrame` and ignores these events. — *Needs a call: letting a provider turn signal drive a user turn start/stop strategy is a design question, not a Settings field, and it needs the SDK pin moved first.*
- **Inert `language` setting** (medium, since 2026-09-01) — `MistralSTTSettings` documents `language` as a transcription hint; under the 2.x session model it is never sent, and changing it only forces a reconnect in `_update_settings`. The 3.x session does take `language`, so the field is now implementable rather than meaningless. — *Needs a call: pass it through once the pin moves, or correct the docstring now.*
- **`target_streaming_delay_ms` not in Settings** (low, since 2026-09-01) — the provider's accuracy/latency knob (80–1200 ms, or 2400 ms) is a constructor argument, though `update_session` can change it mid-session. — *Needs a call: adding it to Settings duplicates the constructor argument, so it needs a stance on deprecating that argument.*
