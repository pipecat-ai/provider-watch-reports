# Fal image generation — 2026-09-14

Nothing to do: the `negative_prompt`/`guidance_scale` PR from the last run merged (#5556), `fal-ai/fast-sdxl` is still live and undeprecated, and the remaining gaps are the same three maintainer calls.

## What's new for Pipecat

### PRs
Nothing.

### To consider
- **SDXL-shaped payload on non-SDXL endpoints** (medium, since 2026-09-01) — `Settings.model` routes to any fal endpoint and fal ignores unknown fields, but `format` only applies to fast-sdxl: all ten other current text-to-image schemas name it `output_format` (flux/schnell and flux-2-pro default it to jpeg, qwen-image/z-image/nano-banana-2 to png), and `expand_prompt`/`num_inference_steps` don't exist on nano-banana*, flux-2-pro, flux-pro ultra or seedream 4.5. — *Needs a call: map the field per endpoint, or keep the settings SDXL-shaped and say so in the docstring.*
- **Default model** (low, since 2026-09-01) — fal's documented line-up has moved on (Nano Banana 2, Flux 2 Pro, Seedream 4.5, Z-Image Turbo, Ideogram V3), but fast-sdxl is still documented with no deprecation or legacy label and fal names no successor. — *Needs a call: choosing a different default is a cost/quality preference, not a provider migration.*
- **Unexposed request fields** (low, since 2026-09-01) — fast-sdxl's schema also offers `sync_mode`, `safety_checker_version`, `loras`, `embeddings` and `request_id`, none reachable from `Settings`. — *Needs a call: `sync_mode` requires `run_image_gen` to decode data URIs, `loras`/`embeddings` are lists of objects, and a fast-sdxl-only field like `safety_checker_version` prejudges the payload-shape question.*
